Bitcoin Symbolism

This living artwork responds to the pulse of the Bitcoin blockchain. Each particle represents the collective energy of the network, drawn together by the gravitational force of the symbol itself—a metaphor for how individual actions converge into something greater.

Desktop Controls

  • Interact: Mouse to push particles
  • Lens: Right-click + drag to zoom
  • Zoom: Mouse wheel to adjust lens magnification
  • Hide UI: Press 'H' key

Interface Elements

Top Right Icon: Access blockchain data including transaction fees and block information. Also Font icons

Font Icons: Cycle through different Bitcoin Ordinal fonts (F) and lock the current font (🔓) to prevent changes during block updates.

Artistic Concept

The connecting lines between particles symbolize the vast network of human connections that give Bitcoin its meaning. Like the blockchain itself, these links represent trust, consensus, and the invisible threads that bind our digital society.

With each new block mined, the particles and typography regenerate—a reminder that the network is constantly evolving, yet the symbol remains eternal, drawing all elements back to its center through an inexorable digital gravity.
